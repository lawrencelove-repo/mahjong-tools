House rules vary

Common Cantonese / HK table patterns. Faan values and minima (often 3) differ by club — confirm with your table.

Payouts

Sum every applicable faan on the winning hand, then read the row below. Base score is 2faan points: on a discard win the discarder pays 2× base and the other two pay 1× each; on a self-draw each of the three pays 2× base. Tables often require a 3-faan minimum to go out.
