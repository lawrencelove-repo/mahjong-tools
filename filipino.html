17-tile hands

Typical Filipino table: five melds + pair. Winds/dragons are often bonus “flowers.” Confirm joker and scoring house rules.

Payouts

Pattern awards on this sheet are in points (1, ½, ¼). Sum everything that applies on the winning 17-tile hand, then multiply by the table’s stake per point.

  • Discard win: the discarder often pays the full total (or a larger share); house rules differ.
  • Self-draw: the other three usually split the payment equally.
  • Flowers / winds-as-flowers and joker rules change both the score and who can win — confirm before settling.
